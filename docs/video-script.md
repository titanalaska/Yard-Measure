# Bootprint walkthrough — beat sheet

**Not a script to read.** Reading prose aloud while driving the app is two hard
jobs at once, and that is what makes speech stumble. This is a set of prompts:
one action, one idea, and a short fallback line if you lose the thread.

**Record the screen with no audio at all.** The words come later, separately,
with no clock running. See "Getting the words down" at the bottom.

**The through-line, if you remember nothing else:**
> One shape. Every answer.

You draw the lot once, and the app answers square footage, snow, and fence off
that same outline. That is the whole product. If a beat drifts, steer back to it.

---

## The beats

Roughly two minutes of screen time. Numbers are the order, not a stopwatch.

### 1 · Open it
**Do:** Home screen. Tap the Bootprint icon.
**Idea:** There is no setup. No login, no account.
**If you blank:** *"This is the whole thing."*

### 2 · It knows where you are
**Do:** Let it open and settle on your location.
**Idea:** It opens where you're standing, so you're not hunting for the site.
**If you blank:** *"It opens where I'm standing."*

### 3 · Tap the corners ← the important one
**Do:** Tap each corner of the lot on the imagery. Go slow. Let each pin land.
**Idea:** Two ways in — tap corners off the picture, or walk them with GPS.
**If you blank:** *"Tap the corners. Or walk them, if the picture's no good."*

> Take this beat slower than feels natural. It is the shot that sells the app,
> and it is the one people will scrub back to.

### 4 · Close it up
**Do:** Close the shape.
**Idea:** That's the lot captured.
**If you blank:** *"Close it up."*

### 5 · The number
**Do:** Let the square footage read out. Pause on it.
**Idea:** That is the measurement. Everything after this is the same shape asked
a different question.
**If you blank:** *"There's your square footage."*

### 6 · Snow
**Do:** Switch to snow.
**Idea:** Same outline, different question — how much am I moving, and where
does it go.
**If you blank:** *"Same shape. Now it's snow."*

> **Do not say it prices the job.** It produces quantities, not dollars. That is
> a deliberate line the app does not cross, and claiming otherwise on a public
> video is a promise it will not keep.

### 7 · Fence
**Do:** Switch to fence.
**Idea:** Same outline again — posts and linear feet.
**If you blank:** *"Same shape again. Posts and footage."*

### 8 · Save it
**Do:** Save the site, give it a name.
**Idea:** It's there next time. Sites don't get re-measured.
**If you blank:** *"Save it. It's there next time."*

### 9 · Send it
**Do:** Export or share the result.
**Idea:** It leaves the phone as something you can hand to the office.
**If you blank:** *"Send it to yourself, or to the office."*

## Getting the words down

Pick whichever you'll actually do. They all work.

**A · One line at a time (recommended).**
Record audio separately, one beat at a time. Nine short recordings, not one long
one. Fluff a line, say it again — only the good take gets kept. Nobody hears the
gaps, because I cut them out. This is how the smoothness gets made, and it costs
you nothing but a few extra minutes.

**B · No voice at all.**
On-screen text over the screen recording. Most people watch muted anyway, and
tool demos do this constantly. Fastest route to a finished video, and there is
no speech to be self-conscious about.

**C · Machine voice.**
I write it out, a text-to-speech voice reads it. Perfectly fluent, zero pressure
— but it sounds like an advert, and for a contractor audience your own voice
carries more weight than a polished one.

**Worth being honest about:** your voice is an asset here. The buyer is another
contractor in Alaska, and a real person who has actually stood on a lot in the
cold reads as a peer. A flawless voice reads as marketing. Option A gets you the
credibility without the ordeal.

## Before you hit record

- [ ] **Do Not Disturb on** — a notification banner is the #1 cause of a reshoot
- [ ] Battery above 60%
- [ ] Pick a lot you're free to show publicly — your own yard, not a customer's
- [ ] Clear any saved sites with customer names in them
- [ ] **Laptop over phone if you can** — bigger, more legible, matches the
      parking-lot workflow

Mistakes in the screen recording are fine. Keep going, do the beat again, and I
will cut the fumble out. Do not restart the whole take over one bad tap.
