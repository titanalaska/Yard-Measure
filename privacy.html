Bootprint — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Bootprint does not have a server, an account system, or any analytics.

Everything you measure stays on your own device. We cannot see your measurements, your sites, or where you have been, because none of it is ever sent to us.

Who this is from

Bootprint is published by Matthew David Walsh ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. It applies to the Bootprint app on Android and iOS and to the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server and no database. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not use analytics or advertising software, we do not use cookies for tracking, and we do not sell, rent, or share information about you with anyone for marketing.

There is no "delete my data" request to make, because we are not holding any.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your work in your device's local browser storage so that it survives closing the app. This never leaves your device unless you deliberately export or share it.

WhatWhy
Measured jobs — zones, corner points and their coordinates, areas and lengthsSo a job is still there when you come back to it
Your site library — labels, addresses, customers, groups, notes, and a site's coordinatesSo you can start a job at a place you measure regularly
Your settings — snow, fence and material preferences, themeSo the app does not ask you the same question twice
The last map positionSo the map opens where you work instead of somewhere irrelevant

To erase all of it: uninstall the app, or clear the site's storage in your browser settings. That removes everything above permanently. We keep no copy.

Location

Bootprint asks for location permission because measuring a site by walking its corners is the main thing it does.

  • Foreground only. Location is read only while the app is open and on screen. The app does not request or use background location, and does not track your movement when you are not using it.
  • Used on the device. Your coordinates are used to drop measurement points, to show where you are on the map, and to estimate accuracy. They are saved with your jobs on your device.
  • Not sent to us. There is nowhere for them to be sent. We have no server.
  • You can refuse. If you decline location permission, the app still works — you can tap corners on the map imagery instead of walking them.

Things you choose to send

The app can produce a snapshot image, a JSON export, or a CSV of your work. Those files are created on your device and go only where you send them — an email, a message, a file in your storage. Once you share a file, it is governed by whatever service you shared it through, not by this policy.

Children

Bootprint is a tool for site and grounds work. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because we do not collect information at all.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. The data the app creates is on your device and entirely under your control: you can view it in the app, export it, or delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it, and the updated version will be posted at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
